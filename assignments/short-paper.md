# Short paper

Write a concise empirical paper, not a diary of your project. The paper should answer one question clearly and tell the reader how the evidence supports the answer.

## Suggested structure

1. **Question and headline answer.** State the labor-market question and the main finding early.
2. **Institutional setting and mechanism.** Explain only the details needed to understand the question and design.
3. **Data and measurement.** Define the sample, treatment, outcome, and important choices.
4. **Empirical design.** Describe the comparison, identifying assumption, and central threat in ordinary language.
5. **Results.** Lead with the key figure or table. State magnitude as well as direction.
6. **Interpretation and conclusion.** Explain what the evidence does and does not establish. Draw only implications supported by your analysis.

Use clear figure titles, notes, source information, and properly formatted references. Cite the original source for every result, fact, and figure that is not your own.
