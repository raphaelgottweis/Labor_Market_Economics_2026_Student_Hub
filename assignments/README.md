# Assignments

Your team develops one empirical labor-economics project over the semester. Each deliverable should make the next stage of the project easier.

| Deliverable | Main purpose | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| Idea pitch | Identify a feasible question and research path early | [Idea pitch](idea-pitch.md) |
| First project presentation | Test whether the data and design can answer the question | [First presentation](first-presentation.md) |
| Final presentation | Communicate a complete empirical argument | [Final presentation](final-presentation.md) |
| Short paper | Present the argument, evidence, and interpretation in writing | [Short paper](short-paper.md) |

## Presentation schedule

| Milestone | Date and format |
|---|---|
| Idea pitch | 15 and 22 October: five minutes, three slides. Slides due 1 day before. |
| First project presentation | 26 November: five minutes plus five minutes discussion. |
| Final presentation | 17 December: 20 minutes per team including feedback. Slides due 1 day before. |

Another team gives feedback on each presentation. Team order, discussant pairings, and submission format are announced on Canvas.
