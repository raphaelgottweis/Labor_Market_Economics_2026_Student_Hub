# Final presentation and discussant role

Present a complete empirical argument: question, setting, data, design, main evidence, interpretation, and limits. Use figures and tables to make the evidence visible; do not read paragraphs from slides.

Each team also acts as a formal discussant once. A useful discussant response identifies:

1. the strongest contribution;
2. the principal identification concern;
3. one missing descriptive result;
4. one feasible improvement; and
5. one question for the authors.

Treat the presentation as a research conference: precise, constructive, and evidence-based.
