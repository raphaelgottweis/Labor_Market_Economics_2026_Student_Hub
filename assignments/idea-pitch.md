# Idea pitch

Prepare three slides. Your goal is not to present a finished project. Your goal is to make a feasible next step clear.

1. **Question and motivation.** State one causal or descriptive labor-market question. Explain why the answer matters.
2. **Data and outcome.** Name the proposed data source, unit of observation, population, and primary outcome.
3. **Research path.** State the proposed source of variation and comparison group. If identification is not yet resolved, say so honestly and name the next action you will take.

End with one question on which you want feedback. Avoid a literature-review slide and long background text.

## Format

Five minutes per team, on 15 or 22 October. Send your slides at least one day before, and name the team you want as discussant. Another team and the instructor give feedback.
