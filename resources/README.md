# Resources and readings

See the [reading plan](reading-plan.md) for what to read and by when. To find a research idea and the papers behind it, see [finding ideas and papers](finding-ideas-and-papers.md).

## Causal inference and empirical research

- Scott Cunningham, [*Causal Inference: The Mixtape*](https://mixtape.scunning.com/): the main companion for the course's causal-inference session.
- Nick Huntington-Klein, [*The Effect*](https://theeffectbook.net/): accessible explanations of research design and causal inference.
- [Mastering 'Metrics resources](https://www.masteringmetrics.com/resources/): empirical examples and supporting material.

## Learning and coding with AI

- Eric J. Ma, [How AI turbocharges your learning](https://ericmjl.github.io/blog/2026/7/27/ai-turbocharges-learning/): recommended guide for using AI as a Socratic tutor and coding-learning partner rather than a shortcut.
- Read the course's [responsible LLM-use guide](../guides/llm-use.md) before using AI for coding, writing, or research.

## Labor economics and policy

- [IZA World of Labor](https://wol.iza.org/): short evidence-based articles on labor-market policy and institutions.
- [MIT OpenCourseWare: Labor Economics I](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/14-661-labor-economics-i-fall-2024/resources/lecture-notes/): optional advanced notes on labor supply, demand, human capital, and unemployment.
- [European Labour Economics teaching materials](https://labourecoeu.ec.unipi.it/teaching-material/): optional material on European institutions and active labor-market policies.

## Data and replication

- [Our World in Data](https://ourworldindata.org/): accessible indicators and documented sources for motivation and descriptive context.
- [Eurostat](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat): European labor-market data.
- [OECD Data Explorer](https://data-explorer.oecd.org/): international labor-market and policy indicators.

## Required papers

The required paper PDFs are provided through Canvas, where copyright and access conditions can be respected. This repository links to openly available resources but does not redistribute publisher PDFs.
Start with the [data sources guide](data-sources.md), then use the [reading plan](reading-plan.md) to prepare the focal paper for each session.
