# Reading plan

All papers with links. Required PDFs are also on Canvas. This public hub links to publishers, authors, and working-paper versions, and does not redistribute publisher PDFs.

Reading rules:

- **Read before class** means a real deadline. **Background** and **Seen in class** need no preparation. **Optional** is for those who want more.
- Before each paper discussion, prepare one page: the causal question; treatment, outcome, and comparison group; the identifying assumption; the strongest threat; one figure or table; one question.
- 17 Dec: read the slides of the team you discuss, one day before. 14 Jan: add your questions for Dr. Johannes Kopf to the shared class sheet (link on Canvas) by 14 Dec.
