# Reading plan

For each substantive paper session, read **one focal paper in advance**. We discuss that paper in detail. Other papers are used in class as figure cases, comparisons, or optional extensions; you are not expected to read every one of them in full before class.

| Unit | Read in advance | Discussed in detail | Additional evidence in class |
|---|---|---|---|
| Introduction | No paper; read the course and project guide. | Labor-market facts and project questions. | Optional labor-market indicators. |
| Labor supply | Eissa & Liebman (1996), *Labor Supply Response to the Earned Income Tax Credit*. | EITC, extensive-margin labor supply, and DiD. | Imbens, Rubin & Sacerdote (2001); Kleven, Landais & Søgaard (2019); Acemoglu, Autor & Lyle (2004). |
| Labor demand | Angrist (1996), *Short-Run Demand for Palestinian Labor*. | A supply shock that traces labor demand; long-run adjustment. | Clemens, Lewis & Postel-Vinay (2018); Card & Krueger (1994); Hamermesh & Trejo (2000); Feigenbaum & Gross (2024). |
| Causal inference | Card & Krueger (1994), *Minimum Wages and Employment*. | Research-design autopsy. | Angrist & Krueger (1991). |
| Unemployment and UI | Card, Chetty & Weber (2007), *The Spike at Benefit Exhaustion*. | Austrian benefit-exhaustion discontinuity. | Meyer (1990); Schmieder, von Wachter & Bender (2012). |
| Active labor-market policies | Card, Kluve & Weber (2018), *What Works?* Read introduction, main figures, and conclusion. | Effects by programme, participant, and time horizon. | Arni & Schiprowski (2019) and AMS-relevant evidence. |
| AI and work | Assigned short note and paper/summary, once distributed. | Raphael's AI-and-work research project. | Brynjolfsson, Li & Raymond (2025); Feigenbaum & Gross (2024). |
| LLM workflow | Eric J. Ma, *How AI turbocharges your learning*, plus the course AI-use guide. | Live workflow for reading, coding, and checking claims. | No additional full paper. |
| Research conference | Assigned peer team's draft and slides. | Peer feedback and discussant response. | Research-design template. |
| AMS guest discussion | Revisit the UI and ALMP evidence briefs. | Evidence, implementation, targeting, and policy constraints. | Student questions. |

Required PDFs are on Canvas. This public hub links to open resources but does not redistribute publisher PDFs.
