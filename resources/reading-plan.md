# Reading plan

Required PDFs are on Canvas. This public hub links to open resources but does not redistribute publisher PDFs.

| Session | Read | By |

| First presentations (26 Nov) | Prepare your 5 + 5 minutes | 26 Nov |
| AI and the labor market (3 Dec) | Brynjolfsson, Chandar, and Chen (2026), *Canaries in the Coal Mine?*: introduction and figures | before 3 Dec |
| Final presentations (17 Dec) | Slides of the team you discuss | 1 day before |
| Guest discussion (14 Jan) | Your questions for Dr. Johannes Kopf, in the shared sheet | 14 Dec |
