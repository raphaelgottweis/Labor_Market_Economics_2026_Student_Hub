# Responsible use of LLMs

LLMs can support learning and research workflow. They do not replace reading, data work, judgment, or responsibility for submitted work.

## Permitted support

- brainstorming and narrowing research questions;
- explaining difficult passages or concepts;
- generating search terms;
- coding assistance and debugging;
- feedback on outlines, figures, tables, and slides; and
- language correction when permitted by the instructor.

## Not permitted

- submitting AI-generated prose as your own final paper;
- inventing references, results, or data work;
- using a summary instead of reading an assigned paper;
- uploading protected, personal, or restricted data; or
- concealing material AI assistance.

## A reliable workflow

1. State the task, context, constraints, and desired output.
2. Ask the model to challenge your design, not only improve it.
3. Verify claims against the original paper, data, code output, or official source.
4. Test generated code yourself.
5. Keep a short record of material assistance.

## AI-use appendix

For each substantial use, record the tool/model, purpose, important prompt or workflow, what you retained or rejected, and how you verified the output. You remain responsible for every claim, citation, result, and line of submitted code.
